The PocketForge cursor themes are derivatives of **Phinger Cursors** by
Philipp Schaffrath (phisch), https://github.com/phisch/phinger-cursors,
licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. The unchanged licence text is in `LICENSE`.

This fork is based on upstream commit
`d8196cd133e5c168adbc84838da673ca0b8593b5` (tag `v2.2`). The original SVG
sources remain under `theme/` in the upstream history.

PocketForge remaps the cursor artwork to its light and dark two-tone palettes,
including a monochrome wait/progress spinner with graded opacity and an outline
ring. It also adds native `dnd-ask` and `all-resize` shapes under `src/`, built
from upstream parts by `src/make_shapes.py`, plus the shape-name table, build,
and tests. These changes are released under the same **CC BY-SA 4.0** licence.
